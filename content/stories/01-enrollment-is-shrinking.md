For most of the past decade, Lynn English High held a little over 2,000 students. In **2024–25** the school's October count was about **2,062**. One year later, in **2025–26**, it was about **1,727** — down by roughly **335 students**, or close to one in six.

A single year is not a trend. Counts move for ordinary reasons: a smaller incoming class, families moving, or students being assigned to different Lynn high schools. This page cannot say *why* the number fell. It can only show that it did, and by an unusually large amount.

Whether this is a one-time step down or the start of a longer decline depends on the middle schools that feed Lynn English. Those incoming-class sizes are the signal to watch next.
