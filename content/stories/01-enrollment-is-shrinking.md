For most of the past decade, Lynn English High held a little over 2,000 students. In **2024-25** the school's October enrollment was about **2,062**. One year later, in **2025-26**, it was about **1,727** — a drop of roughly **335 students**, or close to one in six.

A single year is not a trend, and a one-year count can move for ordinary reasons: a smaller incoming class, families moving, or shifts in how students are assigned across Lynn's high schools. The point here is not to explain *why* the number fell — the data on this page can't settle that — but to flag that it did, and by an unusually large amount for a single year.

Whether this is a one-time step down or the start of a longer decline depends on what the feeder middle schools send up next. Those incoming-class sizes are the leading signal worth watching.
