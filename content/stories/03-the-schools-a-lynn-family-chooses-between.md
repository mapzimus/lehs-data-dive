A Lynn family weighing public high schools is usually choosing among four: **Lynn English** and **Classical**, the two large comprehensive high schools; **Lynn Tech (LVTI)**, a career and technical school; and **Fredrick Douglass Collegiate Academy**, a smaller alternative academy. They show up side by side on the same outcome measures — enrollment, demographics, MCAS, graduation, AP participation.

The catch is that the measures don't mean the same thing across all four. LVTI admits students into structured CTE pathways, so its student mix and its outcome rates reflect a different mission than the comprehensive schools. Douglass is small enough that rate-based numbers like graduation and MCAS swing year to year, and some state figures aren't published for it at all. None of that makes one school "best" — the right fit depends on a student's goals and program needs.

One more thing worth knowing: **KIPP Academy Lynn is not on the scorecard.** As a Commonwealth charter, it's governed and reported separately from Lynn Public Schools and doesn't appear in the DESE district datasets behind this dashboard. A family considering KIPP should look up its DESE profile directly rather than read anything into its absence here.
