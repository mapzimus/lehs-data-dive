A Lynn family weighing public high schools is usually choosing among four: **Lynn English** and **Classical**, the two large comprehensive high schools; **Lynn Tech (LVTI)**, a career and technical school; and **Fredrick Douglass Collegiate Academy**, a smaller alternative academy. They show up side by side on the same measures — enrollment, who attends, MCAS, graduation, AP.

The catch is that the measures do not mean the same thing at every school. Lynn Tech admits students into career pathways, so its mix of students and its results reflect a different mission. Douglass is small enough that rates like graduation and MCAS swing from year to year, and some state figures are not published for it at all. None of that makes one school "best." The right fit depends on a student's goals.

One more thing: **KIPP Academy Lynn is not on the scorecard.** As a Commonwealth charter, it is run and reported separately from Lynn Public Schools, so it does not appear in the state district files behind this site. A family considering KIPP should look up its DESE profile directly — do not read anything into its absence here.
